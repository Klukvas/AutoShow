## Intent

Instagram Reel that sells AutoFlow (autos-sale.com) to car-lot owners. Split screen,
"Директ проти посилання": left phone is an Instagram Direct in dark mode drowning in
buyer questions ("Ціна?", "Ще актуально?", "А є дешевше?", "Який пробіг?",
"Розмитнена?", "Скиньте фото салону", "Ау?") with an unread counter climbing to 243;
right phone is calm — one message with the site link, the buyer opens it, filters
(Toyota, до $15 000), sees the car, replies "Беру". Close: "243 повідомлення / 1 посилання",
"Сайт для вашого автомайданчика", CTA "Пишіть у директ".

## Customizations

- Style A "Два телефони": two iPhones side by side on a dark ground. Left = cold, dark,
  noisy, accelerating. Right = the real site in the Paper Lot theme (warm paper #F2EDE1,
  terracotta #B4453C, Onest + Schibsted Grotesk), deliberately calm and slow.
- Right phone shows the real autos-sale.com (captured screens), not a rebuilt page.
- Copy is in Ukrainian, on-screen text only.

## Notes

- No voiceover, no music bed — the user picks a trending track inside Instagram.
- Reels safe zone: keep key content clear of the right action rail (~140px) and the
  bottom caption band (~380px); the "good" side is the right phone, so nudge it inward.
- No fal.ai / HeyGen generation for this piece. No emoji.
